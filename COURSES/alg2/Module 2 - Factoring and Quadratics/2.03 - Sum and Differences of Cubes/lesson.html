Sum and Difference of Cubes

Factoring polynomials with cubes can seem tricky at first, but it follows a strict, beautiful pattern. Just like the difference of squares, we have special formulas for the sum of cubes and the difference of cubes.

The Core Formulas:

  • Sum of Cubes: a3+b3=(a+b)(a2-ab+b2)
  • Difference of Cubes: a3-b3=(a-b)(a2+ab+b2)

The Acronym to Remember: SOAP

Use SOAP to keep your signs straight:

  • Same sign as the original expression.
  • Opposite sign for the middle term in the trinomial.
  • Always Positive for the final term.
